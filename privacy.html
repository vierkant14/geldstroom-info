Geldstroom: privacy statement

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Geldstroom is a private bookkeeping application. It is built and used by one household in the Netherlands for its own personal finances and for the administration of the owner's own company. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Who is responsible

The owner of Geldstroom, a private individual in the Netherlands, operates the application and is the only party that decides what happens with the data. Contact for data protection matters: kevin.wouda@infosights.nl.

What data is processed

  • Account information of the owner's own payment and savings accounts: account identification (IBAN), account name and currency.
  • Transactions on those accounts: date, amount, name and account number of the other party, and the description.

Geldstroom only reads this information. It cannot initiate payments and does not request permission to do so.

How the data is obtained

Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy (Finland), a registered Account Information Service Provider supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority. The account holder gives consent at their own bank. Consent is valid for at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time, at the bank or by disconnecting the account in Geldstroom.

Purpose

The data is used only for the owner's own bookkeeping: categorising income and expenses, preparing VAT figures and annual accounts, and keeping an overview of the household's finances.

Where the data is stored

Retrieved data is stored in a database on equipment owned and controlled by the owner. It is not stored with a cloud provider and it is not part of this website.

Sharing

The data is not sold and not shared with third parties. The owner may hand over bookkeeping records of the company to its accountant and to the Dutch tax authority, as the law requires.

Retention

Company records are kept for the statutory retention period for business administration in the Netherlands (seven years). Personal records are kept for as long as the owner uses the application and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Your rights

The people whose accounts are connected are the users of the application themselves. They can view, correct, export and delete their data in the application at any time. Questions or requests can be sent to the contact address above. Complaints can be lodged with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
