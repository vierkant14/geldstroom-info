Geldstroom: terms of use

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What Geldstroom is

Geldstroom is a private bookkeeping application, built and used by one household in the Netherlands for its own personal finances and for the administration of the owner's own company. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

Who may use it

Only the owner and the members of the owner's household may use Geldstroom, and only with accounts they hold themselves. Nobody else can register or connect an account.

Connecting a bank account

  • Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a registered Account Information Service Provider. The terms of the Enable Banking API service apply to that step and are shown before you give consent.
  • You give consent at your own bank. Consent is valid for at most 180 days and has to be renewed after that.
  • Geldstroom reads account information and transactions. It cannot initiate payments.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time, at your bank or by disconnecting the account in Geldstroom. Transactions that were already retrieved stay in your own records until you delete them.

No advice, no warranty

Geldstroom is a tool for the owner's own records. Figures it shows, including VAT and tax-related figures, are aids and not financial, tax or legal advice. The application is provided as it is, without any warranty. The user remains responsible for checking figures before using them in a tax return or in annual accounts.
